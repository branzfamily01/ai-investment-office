# AI Investment Office Decisions

## 2026-09-07 — Astra–Sol governance
Status: Adopted for repository governance

Astra is Chief Architect for root research/data/risk architecture; Sol is Main Operator for the majority of work; Codex implements approved changes.

## Existing decision — Workflow before live AI/data integration
Status: Maintain

The product first establishes evidence discipline, role boundaries, risk/red-team controls and human decisions before attaching live LLM/financial-data APIs.

## Existing decision — Human final authority
Status: Maintain

AI roles do not make the final investment decision and the product does not control a brokerage account.

## Existing decision — Evidence and dissent
Status: Maintain

Important conclusions expose evidence, uncertainty and dissent. Missing evidence can block affirmative decisions.

## Existing decision — Selective routing
Status: Maintain

The 27 roles are not all run on every event. Use only relevant specialists and reuse fresh validated outputs when appropriate.

## Existing decision — Deterministic calculations
Status: Maintain

Financial calculations belong in deterministic code/data pipelines rather than being silently delegated to free-form LLM output.

## Scope direction — FX macro/fundamental intelligence
Status: Maintain as expansion direction

The research system may analyze macro drivers relevant to USD/JPY and FX without collapsing the distinction between research and trade execution.

## Change control
Classify important proposals Maintain / Modify / Retire / Hold. Major architecture changes require explicit user acceptance.
